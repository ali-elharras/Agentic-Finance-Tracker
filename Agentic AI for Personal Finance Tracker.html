Turning raw bank text into spending categories

A bank statement only gives a date, an amount and a line of text — there are no categories in it. So the first job is working out what each transaction actually was, using nothing but that text.

What the bank actually gives you

  • VISA PURCHASE WATANIYA PETROLEUM GIZA
  • FAWRY BILL PAY VODAFONE EGYPT
  • VISA PURCHASE TBS THE BAKERY S
  • VISA DEBIT KOSHARY ABOU TAREK

Why logistic regression?

  • Every word in the description carries a weight for each of the eight categories. The weights are added up and the highest total wins.
  • It suits the data: about 1,300 text clues across roughly 1,000 transactions, so there are only a few examples of each clue.
  • The weights can be read back, so the words behind any decision can be listed — a bank has to be able to explain a decision, not just make one.

Fraud does not have a single shape

Isolation Forest looks for transactions that stand apart from the rest. Nobody ever tells it what fraud looks like — it only looks for what does not fit.

How it works

  • It splits the data in random places, over and over again.
  • Normal transactions sit in a crowd, so it takes many splits to separate one from its neighbours.
  • Unusual ones sit on their own and are cut off after only a few splits. That difference is the score.

One tree, one transaction

Three splits leave 6 of 1,240 — and all six are fraud.

Checked against 284,807 real card transactions (ULB / Kaggle). That data is anonymised — no hour, country or merchant — so none of the ten clues exist, and PR-AUC falls from 0.690 to 0.040. The detection comes from the clues, not the algorithm.

How this solution creates business value for Vodafone

The aim is to transform Vodafone Cash from a digital wallet into an intelligent, secure financial companion.

Integrate into Vodafone Cash

  • Analyse the customer’s Vodafone Cash transaction history
  • Answer natural-language questions about spending
  • Provide personalised financial insights

“What am I overspending on?”  “How can I save money?”  “How much did I spend this month?”

AI-powered fraud detection

  • Detect unusual or suspicious transactions
  • Temporarily hold high-risk transactions
  • Alert the customer and request verification
  • Reduce fraudulent activity and improve transaction security

What that is worth to Vodafone

  • Engagement — more reasons for customers to open Vodafone Cash.
  • Trust — proactive fraud protection makes the wallet safer.
  • Retention — personalised insights create value beyond payments.
  • Transaction activity — a more useful wallet gets used more often.
  • Differentiation — from a payment wallet to an intelligent assistant.

Vodafone Cash transactions → AI assistant and fraud detection → better customer experience → business value.
